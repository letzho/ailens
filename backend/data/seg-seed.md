## Key school facts
- AI skills aligned with national standards are embedded across the curriculum.
- All engineering students learn 3D printing in their first year.
- Module exemptions of up to 60 hours are available for students with an O-level Computing pass.
- 15+ industry-recognised certifications are integrated into the programmes.
- Facilities: Smart Connected Solutions Centre; Flight Control & Simulation Lab (Airbus A320 simulator); Advanced Metrology Lab; Healthcare Technology & Innovation Centre; UX Design Space; Service Robotics Development Lab.
- Industry partners (30+): Microsoft, Schneider Electric, Siemens, Shell Eastern, Cisco, Festo, Robotis and others.
- University pathways: credit exemptions and conditional offers from NUS, NTU, SMU, SIT and SUTD.
- Overseas opportunities: internships in 20+ countries, e.g. Vietnam (Eastern International University).
